Caseharden — Day 5 capture
The Notary corroborates a bundle's claims, or refuses to write a chain.

A bundle is a file. It carries the detector's finding, the analyst's verdict,
the approval, and the refusal the Proposer received. Every one of those names
something outside this process, so the Notary looks each one up before it seals
anything, and refuses rather than record a claim it could not stand behind.

1. The real bundle from the v5 run, offered for a version it does not belong to.
   The finding's job and the analyst's verdict both check out and say so. The
   approval does not: a human approved v5, and an approval of v5 is not an
   approval of v6. Nothing is written.

$ notary seed --version v6 --parent v5 --bundle out/run-v5.json
APPROVAL decision ap_8c8f6481c1c3 is a APPROVAL on 'v5', not a APPROVAL on 'v6'. Nothing written.
